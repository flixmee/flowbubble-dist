FlowBubble example

The chat bubble in the corner is the widget. This page answers it with a mock backend that runs in your browser, so nothing is sent anywhere. See also the guest form example.

The embed

This page loads the widget with one tag. On your site, point data-webhook at your own webhook and leave out mock-backend.js.

<script
  src="flowbubble.min.js"
  data-webhook="https://your-flowapp-host/api/automation-webhooks/<automation-id>"
  data-title="Support"
  data-greeting="Hi! How can I help you today?"
  data-suggestions="Pricing|Contact|Opening hours"
  data-accent="#6C5CE7"
  defer></script>

Things to try

Ask about pricing to get quick-reply chips, or send markdown to see the formatting. These words make the mock fail, so you can see the error states: error (500), bad (400), rate (429), slow (timeout after 6 s) and offline (network failure).
